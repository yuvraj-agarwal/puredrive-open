# 03_architecture/

## Purpose

Physical design standards for micro-footprint organic QSR / drive-thru / assembly kitchens (target 600–1,200 sq ft).

## Planned Contents

- Sample floor plans and flow diagrams for drive-thru + walk-up + pickup
- Equipment lists prioritized by necessity (focus on assembly, not full production)
- Ventilation and utility requirements notes
- Modular / prefab considerations for faster deployment
- Accessibility and safety standards
- Cost range estimates for different markets

## Design Goals

- Minimize square footage while supporting high throughput
- Optimize for parallel assembly stations
- Reduce or eliminate heavy cooking equipment where possible (rely on hub)
- Support both drive-thru and digital/pickup heavy operations
- Easy to clean and maintain organic integrity (segregation zones)

## Contribution

Operators and designers are encouraged to submit improved layouts, real build costs, or lessons learned from openings.
