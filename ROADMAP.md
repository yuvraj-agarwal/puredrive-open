# Launch Roadmap

## Phase 1: Foundation (Months 1–6)

- Lock multi-year or multi-season supply agreements with certified organic farms / cooperatives
- Design the constrained menu and validate taste at scale
- Secure organic handler certification path for the planned commissary
- Build detailed unit economics model and sensitivity analysis
- Identify first hub location and 1–2 spoke sites
- Raise capital or bootstrap for hub + first spoke

## Phase 2: Pilot (Months 6–12)

- Build and commission the central commissary
- Launch as delivery / catering / dark kitchen first if useful for process learning
- Refine recipes, shelf life, packaging, and assembly standards under real volume
- Achieve consistent food safety and organic integrity scores
- Hire and train core team

## Phase 3: First Flagship Spoke (Months 12–18)

- Open first micro-store (drive-thru preferred where zoning and traffic allow)
- Hit target ticket times and order accuracy
- Launch loyalty / subscription product
- Collect real weekly P&L data and iterate aggressively
- Prove the model can produce positive contribution after all allocated costs

## Phase 4: Cluster Scaling (Months 18+)

- Add 3–6 additional spokes around the same hub to maximize hub utilization
- Only then consider a second hub in a new region
- Begin selective franchise or licensed spoke model only after systems are documented and stable
- Expand CPG and corporate channels

## Non-Negotiable Rules

- Never open a spoke before the hub can reliably supply it with certified product.
- Never expand faster than quality and integrity can be maintained.
- Measure everything weekly. Adjust or kill what does not work.

This roadmap prioritizes learning and integrity over vanity growth metrics.
