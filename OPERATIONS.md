# Operations Playbook

## Hub Responsibilities

- Receiving and verifying certified organic ingredients (certificates, seals, lot tracking)
- Washing, cutting, portioning produce and proteins under organic protocols
- Sauce and dressing production
- Sous-vide, braising, or other slow methods for proteins
- Rapid chilling and packaging into portion-controlled, labeled containers
- Inventory management and FIFO with short organic shelf lives
- Quality control and food safety systems (HACCP + organic integrity)

## Spoke Responsibilities

- Receiving pre-portioned components from hub
- Final assembly of bowls and wraps
- Final searing / heating where required
- Cold assembly for salads and certain bowls
- Drive-thru / window / locker hand-off
- Order accuracy and speed (target < 180–300 seconds ticket time)
- Customer-facing cleanliness and brand standards

## Critical Success Factors

1. **Menu Engineering**  
   Every item must share the maximum number of components. New menu items are rare and must not introduce unique SKUs without strong justification.

2. **Holding & Shelf Life**  
   Organic proteins without conventional preservatives have tighter windows. Design batches, transport frequency, and spoke par levels around real data, not assumptions.

3. **Assembly Line Design**  
   Physical flow of the micro-kitchen must support parallel work stations. Digital order screens, color-coded containers, and standardized builds reduce cognitive load and errors.

4. **Cold Chain Integrity**  
   Temperature monitoring from hub to spoke is non-negotiable for both food safety and organic claim integrity.

5. **Waste Tracking**  
   Measure and publish waste by component weekly. Constraint only works if you actually enforce low waste.

## Technology Stack Suggestions

- POS + kitchen display system optimized for assembly
- Inventory system with lot tracking for organic certificates
- Customer app with subscription management and loyalty
- Simple QR or digital menu linking to current farm sources

## Labor Model

Hub: Higher-skill prep and production team  
Spokes: Lower-skill assembly + high-speed service team  
Overall labor target: significantly below traditional full-service organic restaurants because prep is centralized.
