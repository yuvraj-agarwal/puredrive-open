# PureDrive Business Model

## Brand Positioning

**Elevator Pitch**  
The world’s first scalable 100% certified organic fast-food system delivering craveable, nutrient-dense meals in under 3–5 minutes at an accessible premium price point.

**Target**  
Health-conscious Millennials and Gen-Z, busy parents, endurance athletes, biohackers, and urban professionals who refuse to compromise on ingredient integrity but still need speed.

**Moat**  
- Supply Chain Lock (long-term certified farm contracts)  
- Micro-Footprint Kitchens  
- Full Organic Integrity Seal + Traceability  
- Operational system that competitors cannot easily copy without rebuilding the hub

## The Organic Trilemma Solution

| Constraint              | Traditional Organic Restaurant | PureDrive Solution                          |
|-------------------------|--------------------------------|---------------------------------------------|
| High ingredient costs   | High COGS, high prices         | Constrained menu + direct farm contracts + volume |
| Short shelf life        | High waste                     | Centralized prep + rapid turnover at spokes |
| Speed expectation       | Slow ticket times              | Assembly-only spokes + pre-portioned components |

## Core Architecture: Hub-and-Spoke

**Central Commissary (Hub)**  
- Handles all raw processing, organic washing, sauce production, sous-vide / slow cooking, and packaging  
- Located within ~50 miles of spoke cluster  
- Must hold organic handler/processor certification  
- Optimized for food safety, yield, and batch efficiency

**Micro-Store Outlets (Spokes)**  
- 600–1,200 sq ft  
- Drive-thru + walk-up + digital pickup lockers preferred  
- Pure assembly, searing/heating, and cold assembly  
- Minimal equipment (no deep fryers or heavy ventilation in many designs)  
- Extreme focus on ticket time and order accuracy

## High-Efficiency Menu Strategy

Constrained menu is mandatory. 80% of sales should come from a small set of shared components.

**Core Protein Bases (max 4)**  
- Organic grass-fed beef (or local equivalent)  
- Pasture-raised / organic chicken  
- Organic tempeh / black bean / high-protein plant  
- Wild-caught or certified sustainable fish (limited)

**Formats**  
- Warm grain / greens bowls  
- Handhelds & wraps on sprouted or organic grain bases  
- High-margin cold-pressed beverages and organic milkshakes / functional drinks

**Defaults**  
- Seed-oil free (avocado oil, tallow, or EVOO only)  
- Allergen transparency  
- Full QR farm-to-bowl traceability where possible

## Revenue Streams

1. **On-site / Drive-thru / Pickup (55–65%)** – Core volume  
2. **App Loyalty & Subscriptions (20–30%)** – “PurePass” style monthly membership that drives frequency  
3. **Corporate Catering (8–12%)** – Pre-packed organic lunch boxes  
4. **CPG / Retail (3–7%)** – Signature sauces, dressings, bone broths sold in-store and through partners

## Strategic Notes

- Never expand spokes faster than the hub can reliably supply certified product.  
- Taste is non-negotiable. Organic food that is bland will fail. Hire chefs who can make constrained ingredients delicious.  
- Transparency is a marketing asset — publish sourcing and certification details aggressively.
