# Unit Economics

These are target ranges for a mature spoke + fair share of hub costs. Actual numbers will vary by market, real estate, and protein mix.

## Target Metrics (Mature Spoke)

| Metric                        | Conservative Target | Stretch Target     |
|-------------------------------|---------------------|--------------------|
| Average Order Value (AOV)     | $15.00 – $16.50    | $16.50 – $18.00   |
| Food + Packaging COGS         | 33 – 37%           | 31 – 34%          |
| Labor (spoke + allocated hub) | 22 – 26%           | 20 – 23%          |
| Occupancy                     | 8 – 12%            | 7 – 10%           |
| Store-level EBITDA            | 12 – 16%           | 18 – 22%+         |
| Payback period per spoke      | 24 – 36 months     | 18 – 24 months    |

## Key Levers

- **AOV**: Driven by protein upcharges, beverage attachment, and subscription benefits.
- **COGS**: Most sensitive to the percentage of premium animal proteins vs plant/legume/egg bases. Direct farm contracts and high volume are essential.
- **Labor**: Wins come from eliminating most prep labor at the spoke and designing for speed so fewer people handle higher volume.
- **Occupancy**: Micro footprint (600–1,200 sq ft) is a major structural advantage vs traditional fast-casual.

## Important Reality Checks

- True 100% certified organic with high-quality proteins will almost always run higher COGS than conventional fast-casual. Do not pretend otherwise.
- Early stores will run worse numbers while systems mature. Model a 12–18 month ramp.
- Platform commissions (if heavy delivery) destroy margins. Prioritize owned channels, drive-thru, and pickup.

## Modeling Advice

Build a simple weekly P&L that tracks:
- Covers / orders by channel
- AOV by channel
- Food cost by major category (proteins, produce, dairy, packaging)
- Labor hours vs sales
- Waste %

Update it weekly. The model only works if you measure reality and adjust.
