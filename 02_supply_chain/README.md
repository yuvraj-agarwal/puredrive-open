# 02_supply_chain/

## Purpose

Templates, checklists, and frameworks for building a reliable, certified organic supply chain that can support multiple independent locations.

## Planned Contents

- Farm / producer contract templates (volume commitments, quality specs, certification requirements, pricing mechanisms)
- Preferred distributor and co-op directory structure
- Incoming goods audit checklists (certificate verification, lot tracking, rejection criteria)
- Segregation and contamination prevention protocols
- Group Purchasing Organization (GPO) operating principles
- Regional sourcing playbooks (starting with US and India examples)

## Key Principles

1. Long-term relationships over spot buying.
2. Certification is non-negotiable — always verify current certificates.
3. Aggregate demand to improve pricing and reliability for the entire network.
4. Transparency: operators should know origin and lot data where practical.
