# 04_operations_sop/

## Purpose

Standard Operating Procedures for running a PureDrive-style location at speed while protecting organic integrity and food safety.

## Planned Contents

- 180–300 second ticket time flow diagrams and station responsibilities
- HACCP-style critical control points adapted for organic handling
- Opening / closing checklists
- Incoming goods and lot tracking procedures
- Cleaning and sanitizing protocols (approved for organic environments)
- Staff training outlines (integrity + speed)
- Customer service and recovery standards
- Incident reporting (quality, safety, certification)

## Core Requirement

Every SOP must serve both **speed** and **integrity**. If a process slows the line without protecting the claim or safety, redesign it.

## Contribution

Real-world improvements from operating locations are the most valuable contributions. Submit with measured impact where possible.
